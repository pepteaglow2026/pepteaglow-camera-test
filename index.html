📷 Peptèa Glow Camera

Capture a proof image for the TEST Order Fulfillment app.

The camera is off.
Choose Start camera to grant permission and show your camera preview.
Camera permission is requested only after you click Start camera. New photos stay in temporary page memory; no photo is sent until you click Use this photo.

Privacy: this public static camera utility does not upload, save, or store photos. The camera frame is captured into temporary browser memory only. When you choose Use this photo, it is sent directly to the Fulfillment window that opened this page. The existing TEST app then handles compression and private Google Drive upload. Nothing is written to the phone gallery or laptop files by this camera page.
